Exercise 6. A Wall on a Quarter Ellipse.

Find the surface area of the wall shown below. Its base is the curve \(C\text{,}\) the quarter of the ellipse \(\frac{x^2}{5^2} + \frac{y^2}{7^2} = 1\) that begins at \((5,0)\) and ends at \((0,7)\text{,}\) and its height above a point of \(C\) is \(f(x,y) = xy\text{.}\)
🔗
The surface area of the wall is .
🔗

Solution.

Parametrize \(C\) by \(\mathbf{r}(t) = \left\langle 5\cos t,\ 7\sin t \right\rangle\text{,}\) \(0 \le t \le \pi/2\text{.}\) Then \(\mathbf{r}'(t) = \left\langle -5\sin t,\ 7\cos t \right\rangle\text{,}\) so \(\lVert \mathbf{r}'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{25\sin^2 t + 49\cos^2 t}\text{,}\) which is \(\sqrt{25 + 24\cos^2 t}\text{,}\) and \(f(\mathbf{r}(t)) = 35\cos t\sin t\text{.}\) The area is
🔗
\begin{align*} \int_C xy\,ds \amp = \int_0^{\pi/2} 35\cos t\sin t\\ \amp \qquad \times \sqrt{25 + 24\cos^2 t}\;dt . \end{align*}
With \(u = 25 + 24\cos^2 t\text{,}\) \(du = -48\cos t\sin t\,dt\text{,}\) and \(u\) runs from \(49\) down to \(25\text{:}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \int_C xy\,ds \amp = \frac{35}{48}\int_{25}^{49} u^{1/2}\,du\\ \amp = \frac{35}{48}\cdot\frac23\left(49^{3/2} - 25^{3/2}\right)\\ \amp = \frac{35 \cdot 218}{72} = \frac{3815}{36}\\ \amp \approx 105.972 . \end{align*}
