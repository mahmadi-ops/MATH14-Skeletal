Exercise 1. Mass of a Cone Along the \(x\)-Axis.

An object is bounded by \(x = \sqrt{y^2+z^2}\) and \(x = 1\text{,}\) and its density is \(\rho(x,y,z) = x\text{.}\)
🔗
The mass of the object is .
🔗

Solution.

With \(dV = r\,dr\,d\theta\,dx\) (the cylindrical volume element (2.6.2) about the \(x\)-axis) and \(r \le x \le 1\text{,}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} M \amp = \int_0^1\!\!\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_r^1 x\,r\,dx\,d\theta\,dr = (2\pi)\left(\tfrac12\right)\int_0^1 r\left(1-r^2\right) dr\\ \amp = \pi\left[\frac{r^2}{2} - \frac{r^4}{4}\right]_0^1 = \frac{\pi}{4} \approx 0.785 . \end{align*}
