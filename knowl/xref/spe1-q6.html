Worksheet Exercise 6.

Let \(C\) be a circle centered at the origin. Also, assume that the vector field \(\mathbf{F}\) is the vector field which is graphed in Figure 3.5.1.
🔗

(a)

If \(C\) is traced counterclockwise, is \(\displaystyle\int_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r}\) positive, negative, or zero? Explain.
🔗
Solution.
Negative. Along the upper half of \(C\) the counterclockwise unit tangent \(\hat{\mathbf{T}}\) points leftward while \(\mathbf{F}\) points right; along the lower half \(\hat{\mathbf{T}}\) points rightward while \(\mathbf{F}\) points left. So the angle \(\theta\) between \(\hat{\mathbf{T}}\) and \(\mathbf{F}\) is obtuse everywhere along \(C\text{,}\) which means \(\mathbf{F} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{T}} \lt 0\text{,}\) and hence
\begin{equation*} \oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = \oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{T}}\, ds \lt 0 . \end{equation*}

(b)

Is \(\mathbf{F}\) conservative? Explain.
🔗
Solution.
No. If \(\mathbf{F}\) were conservative, we would have \(\displaystyle\oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = 0\) for any loop \(C\text{,}\) and part (a) found a loop around which the circulation is not zero.
